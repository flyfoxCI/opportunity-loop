What it is

Kaoポチモ！ turned drugstore visits into rewarded check-ins and reached No. 1 in Japan’s free Lifestyle chart, rising 133 places from 134.

Why it moved

The app lets users check in at more than 10,000 participating drugstores, accumulate points, and redeem them for up to three free Kao products shipped without postage.

What to take

Pair a frequent real-world purchase with an immediate, specific reward and a one-tap action to reduce participation friction.

Wedge

Avoid
