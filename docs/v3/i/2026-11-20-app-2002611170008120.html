How to follow

Play to copy

Map the existing behaviors of a target audience and attach a one-tap reward to the moment they already complete the behavior.

Wedge

Localize the reward catalog, participating locations, and store coverage for a market where the brand's products are already trusted but the behavior is not yet captured.

Avoid

Do not copy a consumer-brand points app without a broad partner network, a desirable physical reward catalog, and a way to verify real-world visits.
