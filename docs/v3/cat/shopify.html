Where to look

  • Target a narrow email-marketing workflow for Shopify merchants rather than building a broad marketing suite: abandoned-cart, campaigns, newsletters, and popups are the validated wedge behind Judge.me Email Marketing.Judge.me Email Marketing is a new entry with estimated $15.8k monthly revenue and 90 reviews; the Newsletters & email marketing lane has 8 tracked apps and 1 new $10k+ app.
  • Look for product-level ecommerce utilities that solve configuration or operational friction, as the largest lane is also producing a newly successful specialized product.E-commerce & seller tools contains 240 of the tracked apps and includes DORO Product Personalizer, launched 301 days ago with estimated $11.6k monthly revenue and 66 reviews.
  • Do not enter as another undifferentiated AI assistant; a general ChatGPT integration is already free, so a builder needs a merchant-specific workflow and measurable outcome.The ChatGPT Shopify app is free and sits in the AI assistants & agents direction; no revenue estimate is available for it, while newer focused products have estimated $11.6k-$15.8k monthly revenue.

How users arrive

  • Use the app store review base as visible proof for acquisition: products with thousands of reviews can rank and convert where newer, focused apps still need to establish credibility.Judge.me Product Reviews App has 50,403 reviews and an estimated $8.82M monthly revenue; Loox has 10,133 reviews and an estimated $1.77M monthly revenue.
  • Make the free install the acquisition hook and distribute around one recognizable merchant job; the two newest $10k+ examples both start free.Judge.me Email Marketing, estimated $15.8k monthly revenue, and DORO Product Personalizer, estimated $11.6k monthly revenue, are both free to install; 323 of 370 apps use a free-plan-plus-paid model.
  • Favor products that can show visible store output, such as review feeds, popups, bundles, product customization, or AI-built landing pages, because these can be evaluated inside the merchant’s store.The top list includes Judge.me, TikTok, Loox, Pop Convert, CWILL Popup Email Marketing, Kaching Bundles, DSers, Snowball, and PageFly, spanning reviews, video, email capture, upsells, sourcing, affiliates, and landing pages.

How they charge

  • Offer a free plan and reserve paid plans for merchants who need advanced volume, automation, or business outcomes; this is the category norm, not an exception.323 of 370 apps, or 87%, use a free plan plus paid tiers; another 41 are free and only 6 are paid only.
  • Price the upgrade around meaningful merchant scale rather than hiding the product behind a low-value paid trial, while recognizing that very high-priced offers exist for specialized business tools.Snowball: Affiliate Marketing starts at $249 per month and has an estimated $1.06M monthly revenue, compared with free-plan offerings from Pop Convert, CWILL, Kaching, DSers, and PageFly.
  • Do not rely on the listing label alone to estimate revenue: Shopify app revenue in this category is rank- or review-based, and should be presented as estimated when prioritizing opportunities.The category contains 303 apps with estimated $10k+ monthly revenue, and every cited Shopify app revenue figure such as Judge.me’s estimated $8.82M, DORO’s estimated $11.6k, and Judge.me Email Marketing’s estimated $15.8k is labeled estimated.

If you build here

BuildBuild a focused Shopify email-marketing app for one painful lifecycle job—starting with abandoned-cart emails plus simple newsletter campaigns—with a free tier and a paid plan for higher email volume or advanced automation.

AvoidAvoid building another generic AI assistant or all-in-one marketing platform; ChatGPT is already listed as free, while the validated opportunity is a narrow workflow such as the one used by Judge.me Email Marketing.

  1. Interview five Shopify merchants or agencies that run abandoned-cart and newsletter campaigns, then select the single workflow they repeatedly pay to solve.
  2. Build a minimal Shopify app that captures one core event, creates one email, and previews it inside the store editor.
  3. Launch with a free tier and one paid upgrade trigger, such as monthly email volume or advanced automation, and instrument installs, activation, and paid conversion.
