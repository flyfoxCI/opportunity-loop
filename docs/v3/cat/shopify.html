Shopify apps

Shopify App Store, 18 categories. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

AppsMini-programs & mini-gamesWeb & SaaSChrome extensionsShopify apps

New Shopify app opportunities are clearest in tightly defined merchant workflows—especially email marketing, product personalization, and review/social-proof collection—where recent releases reached an estimated $10k+/month without yet matching the scale of incumbents.

The category has broad demand but unevenly distributed opportunity: 303 of 370 tracked apps are estimated at $10k+/month, yet only 4 launches from the last 12 months had dated releases and reached that threshold. Newcomers are succeeding in narrow, recognizable jobs such as email campaigns, product customization, and social proof, while broad commerce suites remain dominated by long-running incumbents.

Where to look

  • Target a narrow email-marketing workflow rather than building another all-in-one marketing suite; the opportunity is open because Judge.me Email Marketing reached an estimated $15.9k/month shortly after launch, but the lane remains small at only 8 tracked apps.Judge.me Email Marketing: estimated $15.9k/month, 91 reviews, 128 days old; Newsletters & email marketing has 8 tracked apps and 1 new app above $10k/month.
  • Look for product-specific customization and merchandising jobs where merchants can immediately understand the value; DORO Product Personalizer reached an estimated $11.6k/month with a focused offering, although the surrounding lane is competitive.DORO Product Personalizer: estimated $11.6k/month, 66 reviews, 301 days old; E-commerce & seller tools contains 240 tracked apps and 2 recent apps above $10k/month.
  • Enter through trust and conversion workflows that produce merchant-visible outcomes, not generic storefront widgets; Trustpilot shows that a focused social-proof proposition can gain an estimated $14.3k/month, while Judge.me Product Reviews demonstrates the much larger incumbent scale.Trustpilot: estimated $14.3k/month, 82 reviews, 274 days old; Judge.me Product Reviews App: estimated $8.83M/month, 50,431 reviews.

How users arrive

  • Use Shopify’s existing merchant distribution and make the first result visible quickly: installable/free products can accumulate reviews at scale, so favor integrations and activation steps that produce store-level outcomes within minutes.TikTok is free to install with 15,944 reviews and an estimated $2.79M/month; Google & YouTube is free to install with 5,629 reviews and an estimated $985.1k/month; Facebook & Instagram is free to install with 5,887 reviews and an estimated $1.03M/month.
  • Expand from a recognizable trigger into adjacent revenue tools after merchants trust the core integration; products with an estimated $10k+/month commonly pair a free entry point with paid features, and 323 of 370 tracked apps use that model.Free plan plus paid is used by 323 apps (87.3%); Pop Convert, CWILL Popup Email Marketing, PageFly, Trustpilot, DSers, and Loox all offer free plans or installation while reaching estimated revenue above $1M/month in several cases.
  • Build for merchants with an immediate revenue workflow rather than a generic AI feature; the recent ChatGPT app is free but has no estimated revenue, while revenue-oriented tools such as email marketing, reviews, personalization, and dropshipping show measurable traction.ChatGPT: free, 77 days old, no estimated revenue; Judge.me Email Marketing is estimated at $15.9k/month, Trustpilot at $14.3k/month, DORO at $11.6k/month, and DSers at $1.13M/month.

How they charge

  • Make the free tier the installation and product-led-acquisition layer, then monetize the workflow merchants use repeatedly; this is the dominant category structure rather than an edge case.323 of 370 apps (87.3%) use free plan plus paid tiers; 41 are free-only and only 6 are paid-only.
  • Charge for premium operating features while preserving a visible free entry point; high-performing review, email, ads, and dropshipping products all combine free access with paid monetization.Judge.me Product Reviews, Loox, Pop Convert, CWILL Popup Email Marketing, PageFly, DSers, TikTok, and Facebook & Instagram offer free plans or installation, while each has an estimated $985.1k-$8.83M/month.
  • Avoid relying on low, paid-only access as the entire model: only 6 of 370 tracked apps are paid-only, and a notably higher-priced outlier shows the importance of proving substantial merchant value before charging from day one.Paid-only apps represent 1.6% of the category; Snowball: Affiliate Marketing starts at $249/month, has 212 reviews, and an estimated $1.06M/month.

If you build here

BuildBuild a focused Shopify app for one conversion gap: abandoned-cart email campaigns for a specific merchant segment, with a free campaign limit, paid automations, and a compact setup flow.

AvoidDo not launch a broad all-in-one marketing suite competing directly with Judge.me, Pop Convert, CWILL, and PageFly; Judge.me Email Marketing reached an estimated $15.9k/month, but established review and page-building products already have estimated $8.83M/month and $1.04M/month.

  1. Interview 10 Shopify merchants in one segment about abandoned-cart email and capture the exact triggers they need
  2. Ship a narrow MVP with one campaign type, Shopify-native setup, and a free tier before adding paid automation
  3. Recruit 10 pilot stores and instrument activation, campaign sends, revenue generated, and willingness to pay before expanding features
