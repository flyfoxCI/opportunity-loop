Shopify apps

Shopify App Store, 18 categories. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

AppsMini-programs & mini-gamesWeb & SaaSChrome extensionsShopify apps

Shopify’s clearest solo-builder opportunity is a narrow, compliance- or revenue-triggered seller tool that earns attention quickly and monetizes through a free-to-paid tier.

The category is crowded at the top but still shows room in focused operational niches: 4 of the newer $10k+ apps sit in E-commerce & seller tools, while Judge.me Email Marketing opened a new lane in newsletters with an estimated $17k. A free-entry model dominates—87% of 410 tracked apps use free plans with paid tiers—so builders should pair immediate merchant utility with a clear upgrade path rather than charge before value is visible.

Where to look

  • Look for narrow Shopify compliance and operations jobs where a deadline can drive adoption; Revoq’s EU withdrawal button is the strongest new signal, reaching an estimated $102.5k from 586 reviews.Revoq ‑ EU Withdrawal Button: estimated $102.5k, 586 reviews, free plan available; the lane also contains RF: Barcode Labels Generator at an estimated $123.4k.
  • Prioritize tools that directly support store revenue or merchandising rather than broad all-in-one platforms; the established leaders are concentrated in reviews, subscriptions, bundles, sourcing, and landing-page conversion.Judge.me Product Reviews App (estimated $8.83M), Appstle Subscriptions (estimated $1.63M), Kaching Bundles & Upsells (estimated $1.19M), and DSers (estimated $1.13M).
  • Test email tooling as a focused standalone wedge, because the newsletter lane has only 9 tracked apps and its new entrant reached an estimated $17k with 97 reviews.Judge.me Email Marketing: estimated $17k, 97 reviews, free to install; Newsletters & email marketing has 9 tracked apps, including Pop Convert at an estimated $1.52M.
  • Avoid entering broad AI or social-ad categories until you can name a specific merchant workflow; recent AI and social entries show attention, but the revenue signals in this sample remain weak or absent.ChatGPT and RepOcean Google Reviews are both free with no revenue estimate reported; the AI video & UGC ads and Social media management lanes each have only 12 and 5 tracked apps, respectively.

How users arrive

  • Make the app installable for free and useful within minutes, then let merchant activity expose the need for paid features; the category’s dominant pattern is free acquisition followed by upgrades.358 of 410 apps use a free plan plus paid tiers (87%); Judge.me Email Marketing is free to install and has 97 reviews, while Revoq promises setup in minutes and has 586 reviews.
  • Use marketplace reviews and visible merchant outcomes as the acquisition engine, especially for utility tools whose value can be understood from the listing alone.Judge.me Product Reviews App has 50,464 reviews and an estimated $8.83M; TikTok has 15,951 reviews and an estimated $2.79M; Loox has 10,148 reviews and an estimated $1.78M.
  • Cross-sell from an existing app ecosystem or bundle email, review, and conversion workflows; several leaders have expanded into adjacent revenue-driving functions rather than relying on one isolated feature.Judge.me spans product reviews and email marketing; Pop Convert, estimated $1.52M, combines popups, banners, and email-list building; PageFly covers page building, leak detection, and A/B testing.
  • Build for a visible trigger such as an EU compliance deadline, not a generic merchant pain; immediate relevance should make installation and review requests easier to earn.Revoq targets the June 19, 2026 EU requirement, says setup takes minutes, and has 586 reviews plus an estimated $102.5k.

How they charge

  • Start free, then charge merchants when they gain a recurring or scale-dependent benefit; this matches the dominant category model and lowers the barrier to getting reviews.358 of 410 apps (87%) use free-plus-paid pricing; Revoq, Trustpilot, Judge.me Product Reviews App, Loox, and Appstle all offer a free plan or free entry.
  • Price the upgrade around operational value and merchant scale rather than charging simply for installation; products tied to conversion, subscriptions, or merchant volume offer a clearer path to expansion.Stamped starts at $23/month and has an estimated $1.73M; Snowball starts at $249/month and has an estimated $1.06M, showing that higher monthly pricing can work when tied to a substantial marketing outcome.
  • Use monthly recurring pricing for ongoing merchant workflows, but only after the free tier demonstrates value; the observed examples do not favor one-time purchase pricing for the strongest apps.Stamped is from $23/month and Snowball is from $249/month, while the dominant free-plus-paid cohort includes Judge.me Product Reviews App, Loox, Pop Convert, DSers, and PageFly.

If you build here

BuildBuild a focused EU compliance checklist app for Shopify merchants that detects missing withdrawal, consumer-rights, and shipping disclosures, then offers paid automated monitoring and implementation guidance.

AvoidDo not build another generic email-marketing suite, review widget, or AI ad generator; Judge.me Email Marketing already covers email with 97 reviews, while ChatGPT and RepOcean show no reported revenue estimate.

  1. Interview 10 EU Shopify merchants about their withdrawal-button and disclosure workflow, including Revoq’s June 19, 2026 requirement.
  2. Ship a thin checklist and store-scan prototype that flags one missing compliance element and links merchants to a fix.
  3. Offer the scanner free, then test a paid monitoring tier aimed at stores that need recurring checks and implementation support.
