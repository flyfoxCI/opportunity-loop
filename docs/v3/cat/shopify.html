Shopify apps

Shopify App Store, 18 categories. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

AppsMini-programs & mini-gamesWeb & SaaSChrome extensionsShopify apps

The most actionable gap is in narrow, compliance- or workflow-specific Shopify utilities, while the most established route to adoption is a free install followed by optional paid upgrades.

Only 7 of the 410 tracked Shopify apps launched in the past 12 months were dated, yet 336 are estimated to generate at least $10,000 per month, showing how concentrated this market is. New products such as Revoq, Rubik Combined Listings Swatch, and Judge.me Email Marketing are finding openings around regulation, product-display edge cases, and merchant acquisition. The category’s default commercial model is a free entry point with paid tiers: 87% use that model, and only 8 of 410 are paid-only.

Where to look

  • Target narrow compliance and operational problems that merchants can understand immediately; the clearest recent signal is Revoq’s EU-withdrawal tool, positioned as required for EU shops from June 19, 2026 and installable in minutes.Revoq ‑ EU Withdrawal Button is estimated at $102,550 per month from 586 reviews; 4 of the 262 E-commerce & seller tools crossed the estimated $10,000-per-month threshold recently.
  • Look for underserved, specific catalog and merchandising workflows rather than another broad store-management utility; the only two apps in the Real estate & property lane include a product-option swatch tool that is already performing strongly.Rubik Combined Listings Swatch is estimated at $22,400 per month from 128 reviews; that lane has only 2 tracked apps, versus 262 in E-commerce & seller tools.
  • Prioritize email acquisition and retention for merchants because the lane is small and a recent specialized entrant is already showing traction.Judge.me Email Marketing is estimated at $17,000 per month from 97 reviews; Newsletters & email marketing has 9 tracked apps, with 1 recent app above the estimated $10,000-per-month threshold.

How users arrive

  • Use the Shopify App Store as the primary discovery surface: products with large review counts show that ratings and historical marketplace trust can sustain adoption, especially when installation is free.Judge.me Product Reviews App has 50,495 reviews and an estimated $8.84 million per month; TikTok has 15,950 reviews and an estimated $2.79 million per month; both offer free entry points.
  • Make the free experience solve an immediate setup problem, then expose monetization after installation; this pattern is common across both established leaders and recent launches.87% of 410 tracked apps use a free plan with paid tiers, while Revoq, Rubik, Judge.me Email Marketing, Trustpilot, DORO, ChatGPT, and RepOcean Google Reviews all have free entry points.
  • For a new app, win a narrow use case through the marketplace first, then broaden only after proving that merchants install and trust the product.The market has 336 apps estimated at $10,000 or more per month but only 7 launches were dated in the last 12 months; Revoq focuses on EU withdrawal compliance, Rubik on combined-listing swatches, and Judge.me Email Marketing on abandoned-cart emails, campaigns, newsletters, and popups.

How they charge

  • Make the free tier the default acquisition offer and put paid value in limits, automation, or advanced merchant outcomes rather than charging for basic installation.358 of 410 apps use a free plan plus paid tiers, compared with 44 free-only and 8 paid-only; Judge.me, Loox, Pop Convert, Kaching Bundles, DSers, and PageFly all offer free plans or free installation.
  • Use a monthly subscription when the product delivers ongoing operational or growth value, but expect free-install competitors in the same category, so the paid tier must be materially easier.Stamped Product Reviews & UGC starts at $23 per month, while Snowball starts at $249 per month; both compete with free-entry products such as Judge.me, Loox, Trustpilot, and Pop Convert.
  • Avoid relying on a one-time payment for infrastructure that merchants will expect to keep improving; subscriptions fit recurring needs such as email campaigns, affiliate marketing, social ads, subscriptions, and page testing.Judge.me Email Marketing, Snowball: Affiliate Marketing, and PageFly all maintain free entry points, with Snowball explicitly starting at $249 per month; recurring value products span email marketing, affiliate marketing, and CRO.

If you build here

BuildBuild a Shopify app for a narrow post-purchase or merchandising workflow, starting with one integration point and one measurable merchant outcome rather than a general-purpose operations suite.

AvoidDo not launch another undifferentiated reviews, email-popup, or AI page-building app against entrenched free incumbents such as Judge.me, Pop Convert, and PageFly.

  1. Interview 10 Shopify merchants in one narrow workflow and identify the single recurring task they still do manually.
  2. Build a minimal Shopify-native prototype with one clear setup flow and a genuinely useful free tier.
  3. Publish a marketplace listing that explains the outcome in merchant language, then measure installs and activation before adding paid limits.
