Apps

iOS and Google Play apps, games excluded from the money lenses. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

AppsMini-programs & mini-gamesWeb & SaaSChrome extensionsShopify apps

The best solo opportunity is a narrow, localized utility with a free entry point and a paid trigger tied to an urgent recurring workflow—not a broad app competing on content or features.

Newcomers are reaching meaningful scale: 380 apps launched in the last 12 months are estimated at $10k+/month, 48 at $100k+/month, and the median newcomer is estimated at $38.4k/month. The strongest investigated movers solve a specific local or workflow gap, such as infrastructure monitoring, meal planning, identity access, or professional measurement, while 363 of 380 products use in-app purchases. Treat all app revenue figures as estimates and validate rank gains with install, conversion, and retention data.

Where to look

  • Target recurring jobs where mobile users still need a desktop, especially infrastructure, creative, and professional workflows.HeC reached #4 in Germany’s Top Grossing Developer Tools after rising 99 places; Supanator rose 128 places to #11 in Indonesia; ProPi rose 127 places to #4 in Indonesia’s Top Paid Utilities; TDInput rose 173 places to #1 in Japan’s Top Paid Developer Tools.
  • Localize around a concrete country-specific need rather than launching a generic global utility.Nomor Virtual WhatsApp & WA climbed from #62 to #5 in Indonesia; AOK Ident rose from #67 to #1 in Germany’s Top Free; Gymax rose 174 places to #24 in Indonesia’s Top Grossing Health & Fitness; Eatr rose from #54 to #5 in Indonesia.
  • Build around a daily or weekly action that can become a habit, with widgets, community, rewards, or reminders reinforcing repeat use.Widget Al-Qur’an reached #7 in Indonesia’s Top Grossing Lifestyle with 133,427 tracked installs and 8,790 ratings; mise rose 136 places to #6 in Indonesia’s Top Grossing Food & Drink; Kaoポチモ！ went from #134 to #1 in Japan’s Top Free Lifestyle in five days.

How users arrive

  • Launch through the incumbent audience or platform community that already owns the urgent workflow.Kaoポチモ！ used check-ins at more than 10,000 drugstores and reached #1 in Japan’s Top Free Lifestyle in five days; AOK Ident’s release coincided with a 66-place rise to #1 in Germany; mystc SA rose 77 places to #11 in Saudi Arabia after a redesign and through its own channels.
  • Use a concrete update—compatibility, reliability, speed, or a redesigned entry point—to create a reason for users to return, then verify whether the gain persists.PixieCraft rose 116 places after resolving iOS 27 compatibility issues; AOK Ident reached #1 after identity-confirmation and reliability improvements; Eatr rose 49 places after a faster, less buggy update; Content Manager: AI Сleaner rose 82 places to #8 in Japan after its update.
  • Demonstrate one dramatic outcome with exact setup steps; generic feature promotion is less useful when the acquisition trigger is unknown.TDInput positions a setup-light bridge from Apple Watch and iPhone sensors to TouchDesigner and reached #1 in Japan’s Top Paid Developer Tools; Supanator offers mobile Supabase administration but has no identified acquisition source despite a 128-place Indonesian gain.
  • Pair a completed utility action with a reason to return or share.mise added community features for sharing dishes and exchanging budget-planning tips, then rose 136 places in Indonesia; Widget Al-Qur’an provides Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and reached #7 in Indonesia; Kaoポチモ！ turns store visits into redeemable points.

How they charge

  • Use a free entry point and put payment directly after the first useful result; in-app purchases dominate this category.363 of 380 products, about 95.5%, use in-app purchases, while 17 use paid upfront; HeC recommends free monitoring with paid forecasting and alerts, while mise is free with in-app purchases and ranks #3 in Germany’s grossing Food & Drink chart.
  • For narrow utilities, a one-time purchase can be enough and reduces subscription resistance.TDInput is priced at ¥150 with no in-app purchases and reached #1 in Japan’s Top Paid Developer Tools; ProPi costs Rp 399 ribu with no in-app purchases and rose 127 places to #4 in Indonesia’s Top Paid Utilities.
  • Monetize alerts, forecasting, advanced management, or repeat creation rather than blocking the core task.HeC places storage-growth, cost, and alerting features in its paid direction; PixieCraft is free with in-app purchases and positions upgrades around repeat creation, export, and craft-building; Gymax is free with in-app purchases but exposes no plan or price, so the conversion wedge still needs testing.

If you build here

BuildBuild a localized mobile cost-and-alert companion for a cloud provider used by small teams, with a free health dashboard and one paid storage-cost forecast or proactive alert plan.

AvoidDo not launch another broad AI content, recipe, fitness, or utility app with an unexplained chart spike as validation; these markets already contain products estimated at $740k+/month, including Whipp, mise, TapCook, and VibeShort.

  1. Interview 10 users of one cloud provider and document the most expensive or most urgent desktop task.
  2. Prototype one free alert: storage growth, projected cost, or downtime.
  3. Add a clearly priced paid forecast or alert feature, then track alert setup, payment conversion, and seven-day return usage.
