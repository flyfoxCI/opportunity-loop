Apps

iOS and Google Play apps, games excluded from the money lenses. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

AppsMini-programs & mini-gamesWeb & SaaSChrome extensionsShopify apps

The best solo-builder opening is a sharply localized, single-workflow app—especially in meal planning, developer utilities, or music practice—rather than a broad all-purpose utility.

The category still rewards new entrants: 356 recent launches reportedly generate at least an estimated $10k per month, with 43 estimated above $100k, while the newcomer median is $39.5k per month. Chart evidence points to localization, visible product improvements, narrow practitioner workflows, and fast free-to-paid or paid-upfront models, but the revenue figures are estimates and the available examples generally lack install or conversion data.

Where to look

  • Target an underserved country and a single high-friction workflow instead of launching a generic utility; Supanator reached #11 in Indonesia, #25 in the US, and #31 in Brazil for mobile Supabase management, while its category also charts in Germany, Japan, Mexico, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and India.Supanator: Kelola Supabase; ID Top Grossing Developer Tools #11, up 128 positions, plus US #25 and BR #31.
  • Look for meal-planning products that make budget and waste decisions tangible, then localize around a specific market; the category has 47 recent apps reaching an estimated $10k per month, and Whipp reached the US Food & Drink #3 grossing position only 14 days after release.Nutrition & calorie tracking: 47 new $10k apps out of 316 total; Whipp ($778.8k estimated), mise ($585.7k estimated), and TapCook ($502.8k estimated).
  • Test specialized mobile workflows for professionals and practitioners where one paid or narrowly scoped utility can rank quickly; ProPi moved from #131 to #4 in Indonesia’s paid-utilities chart, while RUB A DUB & CYPHER PLAYER went from #101 to #1 in Japan’s paid Music chart two days after launch.ProPi System Analyzer: +127 chart positions to ID Top Paid Utilities #4; RUB A DUB & CYPHER PLAYER: JP Top Paid Music #101 to #1 in two days.

How users arrive

  • Make a concrete product improvement visible at the exact moment of use, then monitor the relevant country-category chart for 72 hours; PushWard shipped widget and gauge fixes and rose 163 places in Brazil, while AnyREC rose from #60 to #7.PushWard: BR Top Grossing Developer Tools #177 to #14 after v1.30.1; AnyREC: BR Top Grossing Developer Tools #60 to #7 and Top Free #26 to #33.
  • Use a country-specific store page and creator-led demonstrations to make the same product relevant to a local audience; AnyREC had chart visibility in Brazil and Japan, while TranslaCat’s Saudi rank jump supports testing local acquisition rather than assuming a global message will work.AnyREC: BR Top Grossing #7 and JP Top Free #78; TranslaCat: SA Top Free #115 to #16 in one day after purchase-flow improvements.
  • Turn utility usage into a contribution loop by asking activated users to share a reusable plan or tip; mise added community features for sharing dishes and budget-planning tips and subsequently rose 136 places in Indonesia, although causation is not proven.mise v1.1.40 on November 11, 2026; ID Top Grossing Food & Drink #7 to #6, with a 136-place rise reported.
  • Launch with a very narrow proposition and use store visibility to identify which market-category pair is working; Scout reached #11 in Brazil and #8 in Indonesia after a v2.7.0 release, but fell to #80 in the US, showing that localization fit matters.Scout: BR Top Grossing Shopping #82 to #11, ID #8, US #80 after v2.7.0.

How they charge

  • The dominant model is in-app purchases: free apps that build a habit, then subscriptions or upgrades for recurring value. This is the safer default for broad consumer utilities, but the exact plan should be tested rather than copied.345 of 356 recent launches use in-app purchases versus 11 paid upfront; mise, Supanator, Scout, and Widget Al-Qur’an are free with in-app purchases, while their prices and conversion rates are not supplied.
  • For a genuinely specialized utility with immediate professional value, test a modest upfront price and no subscription; ProPi charges Rp 399ribu without in-app purchases, and RUB A DUB & CYPHER PLAYER launched at ¥300 without recorded in-app purchases.ProPi: Rp 399ribu, ID Top Paid Utilities #4; RUB A DUB & CYPHER PLAYER: ¥300, JP Top Paid Music #1 and overall paid #37.
  • Place the upgrade after demonstrated recurring intent: meal planning after a useful weekly plan, database monitoring after a user manages a project, or alerts after defining a persistent search; these products show strong chart visibility but do not disclose their paid offers.mise’s estimated $585.7k, Supanator’s #11 ID grossing rank, and Scout’s #11 BR grossing rank; prices, trials, and paywall timing are undocumented.
  • Reduce payment friction in the market being localized, especially before asking users to subscribe; TranslaCat automatically exposed memberships and gifts after sign-in and enabled annual memberships to be paid monthly in supported markets before its Saudi chart rise.TranslaCat v1.0.35; SA Top Free #115 to #16, though revenue and conversion were not provided.

If you build here

BuildBuild a localized budget-aware meal-plan companion for one market: let users import a weekly grocery budget, generate a plan around stores and household size, and save leftover ingredients; add a community template where users share one low-cost weekly plan.

AvoidDo not launch another broad meal planner, database dashboard, recorder, or screen-recording utility; the evidence shows chart jumps but no proof of broad demand or durable distribution, and mise, Supanator, and AnyREC each point to a narrower wedge.

  1. Interview 10 budget-conscious households or meal planners in the chosen country and identify the most frequent planning failure.
  2. Publish two concrete templates—one around budget limits and one around reducing food waste—and measure template completions and shares.
  3. Ship a basic weekly planner with grocery-budget limits, leftover tracking, and one invitation loop; track activation, shared plans, and repeat weekly use before adding subscriptions.
