Chrome extensions

Chrome Web Store, 18 categories. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

AppsMini-programs & mini-gamesWeb & SaaSChrome extensionsShopify apps

New Chrome extensions can still reach meaningful scale quickly by solving one narrow browser workflow, but only 47 of 563 tracked featured extensions were published in the last two years and the recent median is 3,000 users.

The clearest opportunity is to win with a focused workflow inside an existing ecosystem or daily routine rather than build another broad utility. Recent launches span translation, Roblox, travel search, screen recording, and browser security, with verified users ranging from 90,000 to 6,000,000. Monetization is unresolved in the supplied data, so a builder should validate willingness to pay before committing to paid infrastructure.

Where to look

  • Look for narrow, high-frequency browser actions that can be completed in one click or with minimal setup; the recent lineup suggests opportunities around translation, Roblox workflows, award-seat search, and screen capture rather than a generic all-purpose extension.AnyDoc Translator reached 6,000,000 verified users; RoEarn reached 200,000; seats.aero reached 100,000; and Google Vids Screen Recorder reached 300,000.
  • Prioritize problems attached to platforms users already visit, where the extension can add missing context or actions inside the page; both new commerce and gaming examples use that model.RoEarn helps users preview Roblox items on avatars and earn rewards with 200,000 verified users, while BetterBLOX adds last-online status and server selection to Roblox with 100,000 verified users.
  • Favor utility with an immediate, visible outcome—translation, image editing, security checks, or synchronized playback—over undifferentiated customization; several recent products promise a concrete result in the first interaction.Adobe Photoshop reached 800,000 verified users for background removal and color adjustment, Extension Auditor reached 100,000 for extension permission and malware checks, and ZeroDelay reached 100,000 for synchronizing delayed YouTube live streams.

How users arrive

  • Lead with one concrete outcome in the store listing and demonstrate it inside the exact site where the pain occurs; the successful recent launches are described as narrowly solving visible tasks rather than providing broad platforms.seats.aero searches award availability on Google Flights, Chase Travel, and Amex Travel and has 100,000 verified users; Cat Gatekeeper interrupts social-media use with a hijacking cat and has 300,000.
  • Use shareable, playful, or visually differentiated mechanics to earn distribution beyond direct intent; recent launches combine utility with avatars, cats, games, or polished browser aesthetics.RoEarn has 200,000 verified users, Cat Gatekeeper has 300,000, Geometry Dash Lite has 100,000, and MYNT: Material You New Tab has 200,000.
  • Design for immediate repeat use and low setup friction, because the market is still dominated by older hits and newer products need a narrow reason to become habitual.Only 47 of 563 tracked featured extensions were published in the last two years, 127 have more than 1 million users, and the median for recent ones is 3,000 users.

How they charge

  • Test a free core first, then charge for a clearly separable premium benefit only if users repeatedly receive the free value; none of the supplied recent launches has disclosed pricing, so paid positioning is not yet proven in this sample.All 12 listed recent launches have null USD values and no stated price, while verified adoption reaches 100,000 for several products such as seats.aero, ZeroDelay, and Extension Auditor.
  • For a solo builder, prefer a one-time paid upgrade or low recurring plan over usage-heavy infrastructure, and only introduce recurring pricing after proving frequent value.The strongest recent free positions include AnyDoc Translator at 6,000,000 verified users, Adobe Photoshop at 800,000, and seats.aero, Extension Auditor, and ZeroDelay at 100,000 each; no monetization details are provided.

If you build here

BuildBuild a Chrome extension that searches award-seat availability across Google Flights, Chase Travel, and Amex Travel, with alerts for routes the user watches; seats.aero validates the job with 100,000 verified users.

AvoidDo not build another broad productivity suite or assume users will pay immediately: recent median adoption is only 3,000 users, and all 12 supplied recent launches have undisclosed pricing and null USD values.

  1. Interview 10 frequent award-travel users and document which routes and alert rules they would check repeatedly.
  2. Build a minimal prototype that runs on Google Flights first and captures searched routes without requiring broad permissions.
  3. Publish a narrow landing page and store listing centered on one outcome—never-missed award availability—then measure activated searches, alert sign-ups, and willingness to pay before adding other travel sites.
