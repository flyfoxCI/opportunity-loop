Chrome extensions

Chrome Web Store, 18 categories. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

AppsMini-programs & mini-gamesWeb & SaaSChrome extensionsShopify apps

Chrome’s clearest opportunity for a solo builder is a narrow, high-frequency browser intervention—translation, screening, or workflow enhancement—because only 52 featured extensions were published in the last two years despite 132 reaching 1M+ users.

Demand is concentrated in extensions that save time or reduce friction inside existing browser workflows: translation, shopping cleanup, security audits, and travel search each reached 100,000–6,000,000 verified users. Recent entrants can gain traction with a focused utility rather than a broad browser suite, although the category’s recent median is only 3,000 users, making distribution and a crisp use case essential.

Where to look

  • Look for underserved, task-specific problems inside already-popular sites rather than building another all-purpose productivity extension; focused entrants have reached substantial scale, including Amazon search filtering at 100,000 users and award-seat search across Google Flights, Chase Travel, and Amex Travel at 100,000 users.Knockoff - Amazon Shopping Filter has 100,000 verified users; seats.aero has 100,000; only 52 of 582 featured extensions were published in the last two years.
  • Prioritize workflows where users already feel blocked by language, clutter, or trust: document translation is the strongest recent example, while extension auditing and shopping cleanup address immediate friction.AnyDoc Translator has 6,000,000 verified users and preserves document layouts with bilingual subtitles; Extension Auditor has 200,000 users; Knockoff has 100,000.
  • A useful angle can be emotionally distinctive rather than technically deep: Cat Gatekeeper turns a forced break from social media into a cat-themed screen intervention and reached 300,000 users.Cat Gatekeeper has 300,000 verified users; the recent-extension median is 3,000 users, so a memorable hook can help a small product stand out.

How users arrive

  • Attach the extension directly to a recognizable service or repeated task so its value is obvious from the search result and first use; this approach is visible in translation, Amazon filtering, and travel-award search.AnyDoc Translator reached 6,000,000 verified users; Knockoff - Amazon Shopping Filter and seats.aero each reached 100,000.
  • Use a narrowly defined audience and a specific trigger to create sharing and habitual return behavior; the strongest recent example interrupts social-media use with a playful pet mechanic.Cat Gatekeeper reached 300,000 verified users in 211 days; EXT — Faceit Intelligence and Geometry Dash Lite Game each reached 100,000.
  • Optimize for visible, one-sentence outcomes rather than feature breadth, because marketplace success can follow immediately after launch even without a recorded user change.Google Vids Screen Recorder reached 300,000 users; MYNT: Material You New Tab reached 200,000; ZeroDelay reached 100,000, and all three show recorded change of 0.

How they charge

  • Treat monetization as an unvalidated open question in this dataset and test willingness to pay only after establishing repeat use; no investigated products, monetization moves, prices, or revenue figures were supplied.All 12 recent products have null USD values and empty price fields; the category statistics contain user counts but no verified pricing or revenue data.
  • Avoid assuming that large audience size automatically supports a paid extension: even a 6,000,000-user product may be free, while the most recent products have only 100,000–300,000 users.AnyDoc Translator has 6,000,000 verified users but no listed price; Cat Gatekeeper has 300,000 and Google Vids Screen Recorder has 300,000, both with no listed price.
  • If charging, test a simple paid tier for an ongoing utility after usage proves habitual, but validate the offer with users because the input provides no observed price shape or paying-customer evidence.The supplied category contains 582 tracked extensions, 132 with 1M+ users, and 52 published in the last two years, but no product prices, revenue estimates, or monetization moves.

If you build here

BuildBuild a browser extension that searches and translates award availability across travel sites, improving on seats.aero with one concrete workflow such as alerts, comparison, or a clearer results view; start with a single recurring traveler use case rather than a full travel suite.

AvoidDo not launch a broad productivity suite with an untested paid subscription: the input provides no pricing evidence, and the median among recent extensions is only 3,000 users.

  1. Interview five frequent travelers about how they currently check award seats and identify the slowest repeated step.
  2. Build one narrow end-to-end flow over Google Flights, then add Chase Travel or Amex Travel only after the first flow works.
  3. Release the single-site MVP with a clear before/after demo, then test an optional paid alert tier before expanding scope.
