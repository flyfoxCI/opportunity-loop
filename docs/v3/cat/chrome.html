Chrome extensions

Chrome Web Store, 18 categories. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

AppsMini-programs & mini-gamesWeb & SaaSChrome extensionsShopify apps

Chrome extensions still rewards narrow, immediately useful browser interventions: recent winners range from 6,000,000 users for translation to 100,000 users for specialized shopping, streaming, gaming, and analytics tools.

There is a wide open field for focused extensions that solve one visible browser friction point, although only 51 of 580 tracked featured extensions were published in the last two years and recent releases have a 3,000 median user count. The strongest recent products pair a specific action with low friction—such as translating documents, recording screens, filtering search results, or accelerating streams—rather than presenting as broad platforms.

Where to look

  • Target a clearly visible, recurring friction in the browser workflow; builders should look for tasks users can complete in one interaction and understand from the store listing alone.AnyDoc Translator combines web translation, PDF translation, preserved layouts, and bilingual subtitles to reach 6,000,000 users; Knockoff filters Amazon search results and sponsored listings to reach 100,000 users.
  • Look for specialized audiences with immediate utility, especially language, creative, security, shopping, and gaming workflows, where a narrow promise can outperform a generic feature set.Recent extensions include Adobe Photoshop at 800,000 users, Extension Auditor at 200,000, EXT — Faceit Intelligence at 100,000, and Geometry Dash Lite Game at 100,000.
  • Use novelty and emotional or entertainment hooks to create a differentiated extension only when the underlying workflow is still useful; a memorable interaction can attract attention without becoming a broad suite.Cat Gatekeeper uses a cat that hijacks the screen to force breaks and reached 300,000 users; ZeroDelay reached 100,000 users by synchronizing delayed YouTube live streams.

How users arrive

  • Build distribution around a specific moment of intent: intercept or improve a page, search, stream, recording, or translation task users already perform rather than asking them to open a separate dashboard.AnyDoc Translator reaches 6,000,000 users through web and PDF translation; Alibaba Lens reaches 100,000 users through in-page product search; Knockoff reaches 100,000 users by filtering Amazon results directly.
  • Pair a compact core action with an easy-to-describe benefit, because recent products with one obvious proposition are already reaching six-figure user bases.Google Vids Screen Recorder has 300,000 users with screen capture and sharing; MYNT: Material You New Tab has 200,000; Adobe Photoshop has 800,000 with background removal and color adjustment.
  • Use audiences with recognizable communities and repeated sessions as the initial growth wedge; niche extensions can still scale when the users are highly engaged.EXT — Faceit Intelligence serves Faceit players with win probability, ratings, and pool detection and has 100,000 users; Geometry Dash Lite Game has 100,000 users with offline browser play.

How they charge

  • Favor a free acquisition strategy first: the investigated extensions show user growth but no listed prices, so a builder should validate repeat use before introducing a paid tier.None of the investigated products reports a price or revenue; AnyDoc Translator, Cat Gatekeeper, Extension Auditor, Knockoff, and EXT — Faceit Intelligence are presented without a listed charge.
  • When monetization is added, use it for premium workflow depth rather than gating the basic browser action, especially in utility extensions where fast adoption is necessary.The strongest recent products emphasize immediately useful functions—translation for 6,000,000 users, screen recording for 300,000, and Amazon filtering for 100,000—while no listed pricing data shows an established paid pattern.

If you build here

BuildBuild a Chrome extension that cleans or reorganizes one high-frequency research workflow—for example, filtering duplicate or sponsored results on shopping and research sites—with a one-click action visible in the page.

AvoidDo not build another broad productivity suite: only 51 of 580 featured extensions were published in the last two years, and the recent-release median is just 3,000 users despite the category’s largest products having millions.

  1. Interview 10 users who perform the target workflow weekly and collect five examples of where existing tabs or filters fail.
  2. Build one minimal in-page action around the clearest recurring failure, with no dashboard, account, or multi-platform scope.
  3. Release a Chrome Web Store listing that demonstrates the before-and-after result, then track installs, activated users, and four-week repeat use.
