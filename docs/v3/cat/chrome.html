Chrome extensions

Chrome Web Store, 18 categories. What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

A focused Chrome extension can still reach meaningful scale: seven of the 12 recent featured extensions tracked here have at least 200,000 verified users, led by AnyDoc Translator at 6,000,000.

The strongest open opportunity is to solve one narrow, recurring browser friction point rather than build a broad utility suite. Recent launches show demand for translation, visual editing, screen capture, niche shopping tools, privacy auditing, and workflow enhancement, while the median recent extension has only 3,000 users, leaving room for focused differentiation.

Where to look

  • Start with translation and document handling for a specific audience, because the category leader has already validated demand for preserving scanned layouts and adding bilingual subtitles; a solo builder can differentiate by specializing in lectures, handwritten notes, or one document type.AnyDoc Translator has 6,000,000 verified users; the Tools category contains 68,200,000 users across 34 extensions.
  • Look for narrow browser friction around creative work rather than building a full design suite: background removal and color adjustment reached meaningful adoption, suggesting room for an extension centered on one recurring task.Adobe Photoshop has 800,000 verified users; the Art category contains 5,590,000 users across 31 extensions.
  • Target trust and control for browser users, where security and privacy utilities can earn distribution by helping people understand permissions and reduce exposure.Extension Auditor has 200,000 verified users; the Privacy category contains 90,890,000 users across 32 extensions, including one 1M+ extension.
  • Avoid generic new-tab or broad Roblox utilities unless you own a clearly differentiated community or workflow, because recent leaders in those areas have lower verified adoption than the strongest specialized tools.MYNT: Material You New Tab and BetterBLOX each have 100,000 verified users, while the median recent extension has 3,000 users.

How users arrive

  • Grow through a highly recognizable trigger: users adopt an extension when it activates exactly when frustration occurs, such as opening a scanned document, editing an image, recording a screen, or checking an extension's permissions.AnyDoc Translator has 6,000,000 verified users, Adobe Photoshop 800,000, Google Vids Screen Recorder 300,000, and Extension Auditor 200,000.
  • Use playful emotional hooks to make utility extensions shareable; Cat Gatekeeper shows that a cute, memorable intervention can reach an audience beyond people seeking a conventional productivity tool.Cat Gatekeeper has 300,000 verified users despite being described as a forced-break app that hijacks the screen with a cat.
  • Pair the extension with an existing ecosystem users already visit, because platform-specific additions can scale faster than standalone browser utilities.RoEarn reached 200,000 verified users for Roblox avatars and cashback, EXT reached 100,000 for FACEIT match analytics, and seats.aero reached 100,000 by searching award seats across Google Flights, Chase Travel, and Amex Travel.

How they charge

  • Do not assume monetization is the initial growth engine in this category; the strongest verified-user products in the investigated set do not provide price or revenue data, so a builder should validate willingness to pay before adding a paywall.Prices and revenue are unavailable for all listed products, including AnyDoc Translator at 6,000,000 verified users and Adobe Photoshop at 800,000.
  • If monetization is available, charge for premium workflow depth or usage rather than basic browser access; this preserves a free acquisition hook while monetizing advanced jobs.EXT describes win probability, rating, and hidden-pool detection as free, while seats.aero and zero-delay-oriented utilities solve specialized tasks users can understand without a subscription.
  • Treat any revenue figure for extensions as estimated unless backed by payment verification; the supplied category data contains no verified MRR, pricing, or revenue fields for these products.No price or revenue values are provided for the 12 listed extensions despite verified user counts ranging from 100,000 to 6,000,000.

If you build here

BuildBuild a focused extension for one document workflow—for example, translating and preserving bilingual layout for scanned lecture notes—with a free core action and an optional paid batch or export workflow.

AvoidDo not launch a generic productivity suite, clone-style tool, or undifferentiated new tab; the median recent extension has only 3,000 users despite 53 recent releases.

  1. Interview 10 students or researchers who handle scanned notes and identify the single most repeated browser task.
  2. Prototype the smallest end-to-end flow: select or open the document, preserve layout, translate one passage, and export the result.
  3. Publish a landing page and Chrome listing with one sharp promise, then measure completed actions, repeat use, and signup or payment intent before expanding features.
