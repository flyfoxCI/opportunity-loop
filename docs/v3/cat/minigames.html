Mini-programs & mini-games

WeChat and Douyin mini-games (Gravity Engine boards). What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

Douyin chart surges show demand for instantly readable mini-games, but every observed breakout lacks source-level proof, so builders should validate organic acquisition before scaling spend.

The category is crowded at the top but unevenly distributed: 46 of 110 tracked games are casual, while only 6 are simulation-management and 5 each are match-3, poker, tower-defense, and hypercasual. Recent movers such as 暴走植物园 (#79 to #2), 脑子一团火 (#32 to #2), and 阿皮大冒险 (#77 to #14 on the sales chart) suggest strong breakout potential, yet the evidence does not identify the acquisition or monetization changes behind those moves.

Where to look

  • Test simple, visually chaotic mechanics in a differentiated skin or audience framing; the strongest observed moves reached #2 without evidence of a unique gameplay trend, leaving room for presentation-led differentiation rather than wholesale genre cloning.暴走植物园 rose 77 places from #79 to #2 on Douyin’s fresh-game chart, while 脑子一团火 rose 30 places from #32 to #2 on the popularity chart.
  • Investigate the zombie-search-extraction combination as a demand hypothesis, not a validated trend: first test which level, character, and failure moments users respond to before committing to a larger build.阿皮大冒险 climbed 63 places from #77 to #14 on Douyin’s action mini-game sales chart with a zombie-group, search-and-extract premise; no demand trigger was established.
  • Prioritize differentiated casual prototypes over crowded specialist lanes, because the current boards are concentrated in casual games and offer more whitespace for a distinct presentation than a direct entry into smaller established genres.46 of 110 distinct games are 休闲, versus 6 模拟经营 and 5 each for 消除, 扑克牌, 塔防, and 超休闲; 79 publishers occupy 140 chart slots across 110 games.

How users arrive

  • Build short-video creative as separate experiments and tag every post with its own landing path; compare starts and completed rounds so a chart rise is not mistaken for repeatable organic growth.阿皮大冒险 suggests testing continuous extraction, comedic failure, and repeated-attempt moments, while 音悦节奏球 suggests testing three distinct rhythm hooks; neither product has supplied posts, ads, creator activity, or conversion data.
  • Determine whether a breakout came from product changes, creator distribution, platform featuring, or paid traffic before increasing acquisition spend; rank movement alone is insufficient attribution.无尽火线 rose 64 places from #68 to #4, 音悦节奏球 rose 83 places from #99 to #16, and both have no reported acquisition source, spend, impressions, retention, or new-user data.
  • Use creator-led distribution to expose the core mechanic to narrowly defined audiences, but judge it by completed sessions and retention rather than chart position.音悦节奏球 reached #16 on the popularity chart, and 阿皮大冒险 reached #14 on the sales chart; both jumps have weak causal evidence and cannot yet be distinguished from paid or platform-driven traffic.

How they charge

  • Start with free prototype validation and add monetization only after identifying the audience and repeatable acquisition source; none of the observed chart surges provides evidence about a durable pricing or revenue model.The five investigated products have rank outcomes only, with no reported prices, purchases, revenue, ad spend, or monetization changes; the supplied category data contains no revenue figures.
  • Treat the sales-chart mover as a lead-generation signal, not proof that its current monetization works, because a higher sales rank can reflect paid distribution rather than profitable player spending.阿皮大冒险 moved from #77 to #14 on Douyin’s action mini-game sales chart, but the evidence supplies no sales, revenue, retention, or acquisition data; any app revenue figure should be described as estimated.
  • Avoid budgeting from one-day rank gains; require stable conversion and retention across multiple acquisition tests before funding paid growth.无尽火线, 音悦节奏球, 暴走植物园, and 脑子一团火 each have only one-day chart changes, with no verified spend, user, revenue, or retention evidence.

If you build here

BuildBuild one lightweight Douyin-native casual game with a simple plant-chaos core, three original skins, and three short-video hooks centered on escalating chaos and reversal.

AvoidDo not clone 暴走植物园 or 脑子一团火 from rank movement alone, and do not scale paid traffic on a one-day chart jump; the supplied evidence cannot distinguish product-led growth from bought distribution.

  1. Ship a minimal playable prototype plus three skinned openings, and instrument starts, first-round completion, and repeat sessions.
  2. Record three short videos for separate hooks—escalating chaos, a failed attempt, and a satisfying recovery—with distinct links or codes for attribution.
  3. Run a small creator or organic test for one week, compare completed rounds and retention by creative and audience, and only then decide whether paid acquisition is justified.
