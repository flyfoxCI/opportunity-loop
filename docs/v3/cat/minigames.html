Mini-programs & mini-games

WeChat and Douyin mini-games (Gravity Engine boards). What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

Douyin fresh-game charts show large, fast rank breaks with little visibility into the trigger, making fast creative experiments—not cloning winners—the practical entry point.

The category currently has 140 chart slots across today’s WeChat and Douyin boards, covering 111 distinct games from 80 publishers, while casual games lead the genre mix with 48 titles. Investigated products posted sharp one-day Douyin jumps, including 无尽火线 from #68 to #4, 暴走植物园 from #79 to #2, and 阿皮大冒险 from #77 to #14, but none has verified player, retention, or revenue evidence. Builders should therefore treat these as distribution hypotheses and test distinctive mechanics, short-form hooks, and attributable onboarding before scaling spend.

Where to look

  • Look for an underserved audience around a familiar, easily explained mechanic rather than a new game type; the boards are crowded but concentrated, with 48 casual games among 111 distinct titles and only four role games, suggesting the opportunity is audience and presentation differentiation rather than adding another generic casual title.Category boards have 140 slots, 111 distinct games, and 80 publishers; genre counts include 休闲 48, 合成 12, 模拟经营 7, 塔防 6, and 角色 4.
  • Use Douyin’s fresh-game chart as a live signal for testing whether a concise mechanic can break out quickly, but do not infer product-market fit from one day of rank movement.无尽火线 rose 64 places from #68 to #4, 暴走植物园 rose 77 places from #79 to #2, and 三国志幻想大陆 reached #11 after rising 61 places from #72.
  • Prefer a mechanic that can be understood in a short clip and adapted to another audience, since the strongest investigated examples pair simple loops with distinct positioning rather than established genre breadth.阿皮大冒险 describes a “无厘头宅男” search-fight-extraction loop and rose 63 places from #77 to #14; 暴走植物园 uses a simple plant-chaos mechanic and rose from #79 to #2.

How users arrive

  • Open with the game’s most visually distinctive moment, then show the complete core loop quickly so viewers can judge it without prior context.无尽火线 moved from #68 to #4 in one tracked day; the recommended opening is its doubling moment. 阿皮大冒险’s search, battle, and extraction cycle can be compressed into a 15–30 second clip, coinciding with a rise from #77 to #14.
  • Run acquisition through one identifiable Douyin creator or community channel initially, with unique links or source-level invite tracking, so organic, paid, and peer-driven traffic can be separated.最强小队 climbed from #18 to #4, but no specific creator, post, ad, feature, or launch was identified; the evidence therefore cannot explain the gain.
  • Treat chart rank as an early distribution signal rather than a durable growth metric, and compare rank, activation, and repeat play over several days before committing more budget.最强小队 has only two days of history; 三国志幻想大陆, 无尽火线, 暴走植物园, and 脑子一团火 have only one tracked day, with no verified cumulative user or retention data.

How they charge

  • Validate the free core loop and the first natural paid unlock before choosing a pricing structure; rank movement alone provides no basis for selecting a paywall.阿皮大冒险’s monetization evidence contains no pricing, trial, package, free-content, locked-content, or charge-timing data, and its rise from #77 to #14 cannot be causally tied to revenue.
  • For loop-based games, test tiered offers around completed-round counts and failure pressure so the first purchase follows demonstrated player progress rather than appearing before value is understood.阿皮大冒险’s recommended approach is to let players experience the full search, battle, and extraction cycle first, then place paid unlocks at failure-pressure and progression nodes.
  • Keep monetization experiments separate from acquisition experiments, because the available chart wins do not reveal whether commercial pressure, paid traffic, or creative distribution caused the lift.The investigated chart moves—最强小队 #18 to #4, 无尽火线 #68 to #4, 暴走植物园 #79 to #2, and 脑子一团火 #32 to #2—have no verified revenue or purchase data.

If you build here

BuildBuild a one-mechanic Douyin mini-game prototype with an instantly readable visual hook, a complete 3–5 minute session, and one progression-based unlock; for example, adapt a simple plant-chaos or search-fight-extraction loop to a new audience rather than copying the theme.

AvoidDo not clone a chart winner’s skin or scale paid acquisition after a one-day rank jump; the investigated surges have no established acquisition, retention, or monetization cause.

  1. Ship one playable prototype with one distinctive mechanic and instrument source-level activation for the first 30 users.
  2. Record three 15–30 second Douyin clips, each leading with a different recognizable moment, and publish each through a trackable link or channel.
  3. Measure 72-hour activation and repeat play by source, then test one tiered unlock only after players have completed the core loop.
