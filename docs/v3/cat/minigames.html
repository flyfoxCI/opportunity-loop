Mini-programs & mini-games

WeChat and Douyin mini-games (Gravity Engine boards). What is working here, for whom, and what one or two people could build: guidance first, then the numbers behind it.

Douyin’s fresh-game chart is the clearest near-term opportunity, but treat one-day rank jumps as leads to test—not proof of durable demand or revenue.

The category has 140 chart slots, 111 distinct games, and 78 publishers, with casual games forming the largest named genre at 48. Three products made large one-day fresh-game jumps—无尽火线 +64 places, 暴走植物园 +77, and 过关天才 +45—but the evidence does not identify their growth mechanisms or retention. For a solo builder, the actionable move is to test a simple, visually clear mechanic during a chart launch window and measure attributable starts before scaling.

Where to look

  • Start with a simple, low-friction casual mechanic while Douyin’s fresh-game chart can still provide discovery; 过关天才’s 45-place rise to #20 after being described as a non-taxing storage game supports testing clarity and ease of entry rather than building a complex RPG.The category has 48 休闲 games versus 26 in the next named genre and 12 合成 games. 过关天才 moved from #65 to #20 on Douyin’s fresh-game chart in one day.
  • Look for a distinct audience or core loop rather than directly entering the established xianxia RPG lane, because a top-five incumbent already occupies that position. A solo builder should compete on differentiation rather than ad budget.灵画师 is described as a leading xianxia RPG with reported 千万+ monthly revenue and remains #4 on the bestseller chart despite slipping 3 places.
  • Build around mechanics that can be understood in a short video: 暴走植物园 and 脑子一团火 both reached #2 on their respective Douyin charts, indicating that simple concepts can produce rapid chart visibility even though their causes are unknown.暴走植物园 rose from #79 to #2 on the fresh-game chart, a 77-place gain; 脑子一团火 rose from #32 to #2 on the popularity chart.

How users arrive

  • Make the first seconds of a Douyin video demonstrate the complete interaction loop and add a unique trackable link, so you can distinguish real acquisition from views or paid traffic. Test one creative hypothesis at a time rather than cloning a product’s theme.阿皮大冒险 recommends a 15–30 second screen recording of search, fight, and extraction; it rose from #77 to #14 on the bestseller chart. 无尽火线 rose from #68 to #4 on the fresh-game chart, while 最强小队 rose from #18 to #4.
  • Express social or cooperative value immediately if your game depends on invitations, but judge the creative by qualified starts rather than views alone. No specific advertising or organic channel caused the observed rank gains, so the hook must be tested independently.最强小队’s visible acquisition hook is gathering partners, and it gained 14 places from #18 to #4 on Douyin’s fresh-game chart.
  • Require multiple days of chart persistence before adapting a breakout product’s mechanics; a single-day jump could reflect advertising, timing, or an existing audience rather than product demand.三国志幻想大陆 gained 61 places from #72 to #11, but its cause was not established; all investigated breakouts have only one tracked day of history.

How they charge

  • Design monetization only after users understand and complete the core loop; place upgrade or unlock offers at failure and progress pressure points, and validate them through completed-round behavior rather than rank movement alone.阿皮大冒险 rose 63 places from #77 to #14 on the bestseller chart, but the evidence provides no prices, packages, free-content boundary, or monetization timing. No investigated product supplies verified revenue figures.
  • Do not spend on traffic merely because a product rises on the popularity chart; require a sales-side improvement within 72 hours before committing further budget.灵画师 gained 40 places on the popularity chart from #60 to #20 while falling 3 places to #4 on the bestseller chart. Its reported 千万+ monthly revenue predates the spike, and no campaign is documented.

If you build here

BuildBuild one low-friction Douyin mini-game this month: a simple collection or organization loop that can be understood and completed in a single short clip, with a free core round and one clearly timed paid unlock.

AvoidDo not clone 灵画师’s xianxia RPG positioning or scale ad spend from a popularity-chart jump; the incumbent was #4 on the bestseller chart while its popularity rank rose to #20 without matching sales movement.

  1. Write one mechanic sentence and a 15-second opening clip showing the complete loop, following the demonstrated clarity used for 阿皮大冒险.
  2. Publish one measured Douyin release with a unique link and compare qualified starts, completed rounds, and chart movement over at least three days.
  3. Add one low-friction paid unlock at a clear progression or failure point, then keep spending disabled until both starter retention and a sales-side signal are visible.
