The line a product must cross

A product qualifies when today is at least 3× its own average over the previous 7 days, above an absolute floor. New sources have no history yet: until a product has 3 days in our data, we use the change figure the source itself reports and label the card source delta or cold start instead of own baseline.

Picking what to brief

score = strength × publisher penalty × freshness × (0.5 + direction fit) × (1 + demand echo) × novelty. Grossing-chart moves weigh 1.4×, paid-chart moves 0.45× (a few sales move a thin paid chart); products launched in the last year weigh 1.3×, older than three years 0.55×. Big platforms are penalised, products briefed in the last 14 days are skipped, each surface gets at least 2 briefs and at most 40% of the 24 daily slots.

Two modes of brief

Data-only (always)

Every field is computed from collected data: badge, curve, charts, measurements, dated events, peers, Demand Echo, discounts, grade, market-gap wedge. The headline describes the move; the trigger reads “not checked” unless evidence exists.

Agent-checked (CI with keys)

TinyFish searches the web for the trigger; an LLM writes the lesson headline, positioning, differentiators, moat, wedge and Build Kit from the gathered evidence only. A verify step downgrades any “fact” whose link we didn't gather to “inferred”. Badges and grades stay computed by code.
