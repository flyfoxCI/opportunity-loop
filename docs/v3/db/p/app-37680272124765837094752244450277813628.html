What it is

HeC is an independent native iOS client for Hetzner Cloud. The listing says it monitors servers, manages DNS, creates resources, and supports incident response from anywhere, with German and English availability.

Where users come from

The product is available in Germany’s App Store Developer Tools category and also appears in the country’s Top Free chart at #66. The evidence does not identify a specific post, community launch, ad campaign, or storefront feature that supplied users.

How it makes money

The app is listed as free with in-app purchases. Its placement in Germany’s Top Grossing Developer Tools chart confirms a paid-chart position, but no plan names, prices, trial details, revenue, or conversion rate are provided.

Who built it

Dorian Borovina is identified as the developer, and the product page links to the developer’s site. The listing describes HeC as an independent third-party app with no connection to Hetzner.

What it does badly

The evidence does not document product failures. The clearest limitation is evidentiary: only 11 ratings are visible, there are no recent ratings, and only two days of the product’s own history are tracked.

If it were you

Start with one paid workflow—alerting users to storage growth and projected cost—instead of rebuilding every infrastructure control. Validate it with Hetzner users, then track weekly usage, paid conversion, and retention; the chart jump alone would not prove that the product has durable demand.
