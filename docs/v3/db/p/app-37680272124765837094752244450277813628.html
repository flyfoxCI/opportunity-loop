what it is

HeC is an independent native SwiftUI iOS client for Hetzner Cloud. It lets users monitor servers, manage DNS, create resources, and respond to infrastructure problems; it is available in German and English and states that Hetzner has no official iOS Cloud app.

where users come from

The evidence does not identify the acquisition source. HeC first appeared on a tracked chart on 2026-11-19 and later reached rank 6 in Germany’s Developer Tools grossing chart, but the App Store evidence provides no install or referral data.

how it makes money

HeC is listed as free and supports in-app purchases, but the supplied evidence does not state the plan, price, trial, or paid features. It is therefore not possible to explain when or how money was taken.

who built it

HeC was built by Dorian Borovina. The product listing identifies it as an independent third-party app and provides the developer site https://dorian.borovina.com.

what it does badly

The evidence does not document user complaints or product failures. It also does not show broad adoption: the grossing rank improved by 97 places while the free rank fell by 32 places, so the chart movement was narrow rather than across both lanes.

if it were you

HeC suits developers who already use Hetzner Cloud and want native mobile visibility, especially around storage growth and cost forecasting. Start with a narrowly scoped iOS prototype for one costly blind spot, then test activation and retention; this move would be wrong if the paid or free-chart gains do not persist beyond two days of history (inferred).
