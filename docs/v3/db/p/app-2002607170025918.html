What it is

PixieCraft is a free iOS app for creators, crafters, and collectors. It converts photos into pixel-art patterns, supports blank-canvas design, and connects the design process to bead-by-bead craft work.

Where users come from

The evidence does not identify the exact acquisition source. The app was first seen on a tracked chart on November 18, 2026, and later rose to No. 20 in Japan’s Top Grossing Shopping chart.

How it makes money

The app is free with in-app purchases. Its store listing does not provide prices, plan shapes, trial terms, or which features are paid, so the monetization details are unclear.

Who built it

PixieCraft is listed by Submartingale Technology Co., Ltd. It was released on July 16, 2026.

What it does badly

Before version 1.5.6, compatibility issues persisted on iOS 27 and above. The evidence also lacks proof that the chart jump came from the update, and the app’s broader acquisition and retention performance are unknown.

If it were you

This case suits creators serving pixel-art and bead-craft users who want an integrated workflow. Start by testing one photo-to-pattern-to-craft task; you would be wrong if affected users do not complete that task more often after the compatibility fix or do not return to repeat it.
