What it is

Supanator is a native Supabase client for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. It combines table and row administration, SQL execution, relationship diagrams, Row Level Security management, project monitoring, and push downtime alerts. It was released on 2025-07-30 and is free to download with in-app purchases.

Where users come from

The evidence does not identify the users’ acquisition source. Supanator charts in BR, DE, ID, JP, and US, but no attributable post, advertisement, featuring, or platform launch was found. The 128-place Indonesian rise therefore cannot be tied to a specific channel.

How it makes money

The App Store listing says the app is free and supports in-app purchases, but no subscription plan or price is provided. Its appearance in Top Grossing charts indicates monetization, not the amount collected or when users pay.

Who built it

Layton Labs AB is listed as the developer. No founder background, existing audience, portfolio, funding, or launch resources are provided.

What it does badly

The tracked update on 2026-11-15 contained only “Bug fixes,” so no meaningful feature expansion is evident. The app also does not chart in the direction’s IN, MX, SA, and TR markets, leaving room for localized versions; no product-quality complaints were found.

If it were you

Do not clone the whole client. Pick one mobile workflow, validate it with developers in five interviews, launch a narrow native prototype, and measure completed tasks through referral links. This would be the right fit for builders who can reach Supabase users and solve a frequent task in under two weeks. It would be wrong if the demand for phone-based database administration proves rare or if the chart jump was driven by revenue rather than sustained user acquisition.
