What it is

Supanator is a native Supabase client for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. It supports table browsing and editing, SQL and query building, relationship diagrams, Row Level Security policies, project monitoring, and outage push notifications.

Where users come from

The exact acquisition source is not documented. The app first appeared on a tracked chart on 2026-11-18 and was visible across grossing and free developer-tools rankings in Indonesia, the US, Brazil, Japan, and Germany; no ads, posts, creator campaigns, or store featuring were found.

How it makes money

The App Store listing says the app is free and includes in-app purchases, but it does not provide prices, subscriptions, trials, or which capabilities are locked. It nevertheless appeared in grossing rankings, although revenue totals are unknown.

Who built it

The listed developer is Layton Labs AB. The app was released on 2025-07-30 and its website is supanator.com.

What it does badly

The evidence shows only 1.0 rating and one tracked day, so product quality cannot be judged reliably. The only identified release was labeled “Bug fixes.” on 2026-11-15, and the product’s charting behavior is stronger in some countries than others; the cause of that distribution gap is not found.

If it were you

Use the case for builders with Supabase projects and Apple devices, start with table viewing plus SQL execution, and validate whether monitoring and policy management become repeatable paid jobs. Supanator's 128-place Indonesian ranking rise is a signal worth testing, not proof it worked: it would be wrong if the movement reflects only a small number of installs, a short-lived chart effect, or revenue rather than broad adoption.
