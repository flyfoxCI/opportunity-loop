What it is

ThriftLens is an iOS app for thrift shoppers and resellers. Users photograph an item with the camera and receive AI-assisted brand recognition, resale-value, and profit-potential analysis, with scans saved to a personal dashboard.

If it were you

Start with one resale market and one high-frequency decision: whether a thrift find will make money after fees and shipping. Validate with 50 real items, compare estimates with realized prices, and consider a free first scan followed by paid unlimited scans and alerts. The move would be disproven if users repeatedly distrust the estimates or will not return to scan additional finds.
