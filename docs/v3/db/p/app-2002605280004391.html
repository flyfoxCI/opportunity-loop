What it is

PushWard is an iOS developer tool that turns plain HTTP requests into Live Activities shown in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen. Its flow is to create an activity with a template and slug, then update state and content with a PATCH request, without managing APNs certificates or device tokens. It includes eight built-in templates.

Where users come from

The acquisition source is not established. The product was first seen on a tracked chart on 2026-11-19 after its v1.30.1 release on 2026-11-16; the evidence does not show where its users actually came from.

What it does badly
