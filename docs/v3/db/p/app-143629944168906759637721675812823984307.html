What it is

Eatr is a free iOS recipe and healthy-meal companion. Its description says users can choose a cooking window, from 15-minute bites to meals under an hour, and have the app plan a menu; the app has in-app purchases and is available in Brazil.

Where users come from

The verified signal is chart movement rather than an identifiable acquisition campaign. Eatr first appeared on a tracked chart on 2026-11-18 and later rose 49 places to No. 5 in Indonesia's Top Grossing Food & Drink chart. No ads, social posts, featuring, or store-search evidence is available, so the source of installs is not established.

How it makes money

The app is free to download and has in-app purchases, but no subscription price, paywall, trial, or purchase timing is shown in the evidence. The Top Grossing chart indicates monetization activity, but it does not disclose Eatr's revenue or the amount paid by any user.

Who built it

The App Store listing identifies the developer as Eatr. The app was released on 2024-04-01 and had 959 ratings with a 4.8 average at the measured point.

What it does badly

The evidence does not identify a specific product defect. It does show that the chart lift was concentrated in Indonesia, while Brazil, Germany, Japan, and the United States changed by much smaller amounts or declined. The release itself was described only as small improvements, with no new acquisition feature identified.

If it were you

Copy the release discipline, not the recipe category: make one measurable product improvement, test one market where the app has no current chart presence, and localize recipes and onboarding. It suits solo builders who can create credible localized food content. Start with five recipes and a waitlist in one country; this approach would be disproven if localized visitors do not sign up or save menus at rates comparable to the original market.
