What it is

Scout: Marketplace Alerts is a free iPhone app with in-app purchases that watches Marketplace listings and notifies users when matching items appear. It supports multi-location searches and says AI matching filters spam, off-topic results, and repeat relists. [https://apps.apple.com/id/app/scout-marketplace-alerts/id6738621889?uo=4]

Where users come from

The supplied evidence does not identify user acquisition channels. It shows chart visibility instead: Scout rose from #82 to #11 in Brazil’s Top Grossing · Shopping chart and ranked #8 in Indonesia; these positions are not download counts. [https://apps.apple.com/id/app/scout-marketplace-alerts/id6738621889?uo=4]

How it makes money

Scout is free with in-app purchases, but the supplied evidence gives no price, subscription shape, trial, paywall, or purchase trigger. Monetization is therefore confirmed only at the product-format level. [https://apps.apple.com/id/app/scout-marketplace-alerts/id6738621889?uo=4]

Who built it

The App Store record names Matten Pty Ltd as the developer and dates the release to 2025-04-05. [https://apps.apple.com/id/app/scout-marketplace-alerts/id6738621889?uo=4]

What it does badly

Its geography performance is uneven: it ranked #11 in Brazil and #8 in Indonesia but #80 in the US. The evidence also does not establish whether the v2.7.0 update caused the Brazil jump, and the broader marketplace pain visible in Reddit threads is not direct evidence of Scout demand. [https://apps.apple.com/id/app/scout-marketplace-alerts/id6738621889?uo=4]

If it were you

Scout suits marketplace shoppers tired of refreshing and searching across locations. Start with one marketplace and one concrete search type, then validate installs, retained alert sessions, and paid conversion. It would be wrong to treat a 71-place chart jump as proof of broad or durable demand. [https://apps.apple.com/id/app/scout-marketplace-alerts/id6738621889?uo=4]
