What it is

ProPi System Analyzer is an iPhone utility for sound engineers, technicians, and live sound professionals. It brings real-time audio measurement and system-alignment tools to mobile workflows, including RTA, spectrum, phase, magnitude, impulse response, group delay, SPL, delay, time alignment, and sweeps.
