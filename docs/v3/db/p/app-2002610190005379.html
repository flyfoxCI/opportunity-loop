What it is

ProPi System Analyzer is an iPhone utility for professional audio measurement and system alignment. Its listed tools include RTA, spectrum analysis, phase and magnitude measurement, impulse response, group delay, SPL monitoring, and delay and alignment tools.

Where users come from

The app is positioned for sound engineers, technicians, and live sound professionals, but no specific acquisition post, ad, campaign, community, or referral source is found. The evidenced signal is its movement from #131 to #4 in Indonesia’s Top Paid Utilities chart.

How it makes money

The app is sold in Indonesia for Rp 399ribu and has no in-app purchases listed. It was also #56 in Indonesia’s Top Paid chart, although revenue and download counts are not available.
