What it is

Organize fotos: FocusClean is a Brazilian-localized iPhone utility for cleaning photos and videos. It finds similar photos, blurry images, and screenshots, groups photos by month, and uses swipes to archive or delete them.

Where users come from

The app appeared first on a tracked chart on 2026-11-18 and later reached #15 on Brazil's Top Grossing Utilities chart, rising 85 places from #100 since yesterday. No post, ad, app hunt, feature, or other acquisition source was found, and a chart rank is not an install count.

How it makes money

The app is free with in-app purchases. Its description says users may organize up to 100 photos per month for free; exact paid-plan prices, locked features, trials, and purchase timing were not found.

Who built it

The App Store lists Ultra Logic Limited as the developer. No founder name, team size, prior portfolio, funding, or relevant audience resources were provided.

What it does badly

The evidence does not show whether cleanup is accurate, whether the 100-photo allowance is restrictive, how destructive actions are protected, or whether the paid offer is clear. The only supplied documentation link concerns an unrelated CLI and cannot support the product story.

If it were you

Start by validating a Portuguese cleanup workflow with users whose libraries contain duplicates, blur, and screenshots; ship monthly grouping, swipe actions, and undo, then offer a paid tier after a completed cleanup. It suits iPhone users who want fast photo cleanup, but this verdict is provisional: the sudden chart trigger is unknown, and the rank must be checked against installs, conversion, retention, and revenue before assuming the model took off.
