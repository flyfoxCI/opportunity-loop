What it is

Kaoポチモ！ is a free, official Kao app for earning points by checking in at drugstores. Users can exchange 15 points for a choice of more than 10 Kao products, with up to three physical items delivered free of shipping.

Where users come from

The app launched on 2026-11-15 and shipped v1.0 the same day. It later rose 133 places to #1 in Japan's Top Free Lifestyle chart, but no specific post, ad, campaign, or store-driven acquisition source is documented.

How it makes money

The app is free and has no in-app purchase listed. Its visible exchange model converts check-in points into Kao products rather than charging users directly; the commercial value to Kao is not quantified.

Who built it

Kao Corporation built the app. It uses Kao's own product catalog and a network covering more than 10,000 drugstores, including Welcia, Tsuruha, Sundrug, Sugi, Matsumoto Kiyoshi, and Cocokara Fine.

What it does badly

The evidence does not document failures, but several mechanics remain unclear: how users are identified in-store, how fraudulent check-ins are prevented, how rewards are fulfilled, and why people return after the first check-in.

If it were you

The play suits a solo builder with an existing audience and access to trusted partner locations, but not a brand-independent imitation. Start by validating that users will press one button during a purchase, then track first check-in, first redemption, and second visit; rankings alone would not prove the model works.
