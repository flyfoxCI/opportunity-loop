Why it moved

Kaoポチモ！ launched on 2026-11-15 and rose 133 chart places, from 134 to No. 1 in JP Top Free · Lifestyle, within five days. The concrete mechanic is a one-tap check-in across more than 10,000 stores that earns free, shipped Kao products; however, the evidence does not show the acquisition channel or prove that the feature itself caused the ranking jump.

Direction

Map one purchase behavior and attach a points-based reward to visits that shoppers already make.

The reward reinforces an existing shopping habit while the app provides the missing check-in and point-tracking layer.

Kao launched the app on 2026-11-15 with check-ins at more than 10,000 participating drugstores; 15 points unlock a choice of more than 10 Kao products, with exchanges capped at three products and free shipping.

Result Within five days, the app moved from chart position 134 to No. 1 in JP Top Free · Lifestyle; this is a chart movement, not a download count, and no causal demand event was documented. Likely cause

Growth

Launch through the merchants and audience already surrounding the rewarded purchase.

Existing store traffic can expose the app exactly when the visit behavior needed to earn its reward occurs.

The app targeted more than 10,000 drugstores, including ウエルシア薬局, ツルハドラッグ, サンドラッグ, スギ薬局, マツモトキヨシ, and ココカラファイン; the evidence does not identify a launch post, ad, store featuring, roundup, or platform launch.

Result By five days after launch, the app reached No. 1 in JP Top Free · Lifestyle after a 133-place rise; the source of the users is not found and the chart does not reveal how many were new installs. Cause unproven

Money

Keep core participation free while tying a valuable reward to a threshold and redeeming it through the merchant’s fulfillment system.

The free utility encourages adoption, while the reward threshold creates a repeatable reason to return and grounds value in products the operator can supply.

The app is free with no in-app purchase. Users accumulate visit and shopping points, and at 15 points can exchange them for up to three Kao products; the description states that shipping is free.

Result The product charted No. 1 in JP Top Free · Lifestyle five days after launch, but no revenue, redemption, or conversion figures are provided. Cause unproven

If it were you

DoDo not clone the nationwide store network; begin with one merchant cluster where the founder already has audience access and can observe repeat behavior. The evidence leaves the acquisition channel and scale of adoption open, and it does not establish whether the app has sustainable daily engagement.

CopyAttach a one-tap visit action to an existing purchase journey and make the reward visible after the first participation.

AvoidDo not copy a mass-store footprint before proving repeat check-ins, or interpret the Lifestyle rank as proof of mass-market scale.

What it is

Kaoポチモ！ is Kao Corporation’s official rewards app for drugstore visits. Users check in with one tap at more than 10,000 participating stores, accumulate visit and shopping points, and exchange 15 points for a choice of full Kao products, with no postage charge.

Where users come from

The evidence names a broad participating retail network but does not identify how users found the app. No launch post, advertisement, App Store featuring, roundup, or platform launch is documented, so the acquisition source is not found.

How it makes money

The app is free and has no in-app purchase. Its exchangeable value comes from Kao products and its operating role in the company’s wider consumer and retail ecosystem; no direct revenue figure is supplied.

Who built it

The developer is Kao Corporation. The app launched on 2026-11-15 and shipped a v1.0 update on the same date.

What it does badly

The evidence does not demonstrate which acquisition channel drove the surge, whether users check in repeatedly, or whether rewards are actually redeemed. A jump to No. 1 in JP Top Free · Lifestyle is a ranking signal, not evidence of a specific download volume.

If it were you

Kaoポチモ！ suits frequent drugstore shoppers who already want Kao products. A builder should start with one merchant cluster, test a one-tap check-in and a concrete reward, and measure repeat check-ins and redemption; the launch should be considered wrong if most participants install once, earn nothing, or never redeem.
