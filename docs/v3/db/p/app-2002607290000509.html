The product itselfTiming / platform event

Direction

Identify a dated release, shutdown, pricing change, or neglected workflow that creates immediate demand, then ship one redesigned workflow around it within seven days.

A visible change timed close to a demand event is more likely to prompt store visitors to download than an undifferentiated maintenance release.

On 2026-11-18, STC shipped mystc SA version 5.0.19 with a new Home, dedicated Line Hub, refreshed Store, loyalty programs, saved locations, themes, and smoother actions; its release notes began, “Welcome to the all-new mystc app, reimagined to introduce a new era for mystc with a fully redesigned experience.”

Result By the next daily observation, the app was rank 7 on SA Top Free versus rank 88 before the update, an 81-place increase; this was a rank change, not a download count or a cumulative-growth total. Likely cause

In the playbook: Survey one underserved audience, identify the same recurring decision or workaround in at least five recent responses, then ship one focused workflow for that exact task and measure its adoption for 14 days. →

Growth

Trace the first 30 users to a named post, reply, video, ad, store-search term, roundup, or platform feature before assuming a product update drove acquisition.

Separating an existing audience effect from update-driven demand prevents a rank spike from being misattributed to the product change.
