What it is

mise is a free meal-planning app that creates personalized plans from dietary preferences, calorie goals, preferred supermarkets, budget, and ingredients already at home. It targets weekly preparation, healthier eating, lower shopping costs, and reduced food waste.

Where users come from

The acquisition source is not documented. The app first appeared on a tracked chart on 2026-11-18 and later rose 136 places to No. 6 on Indonesia’s Top Grossing Food & Drink chart; this does not identify which users or channels drove the change.

How it makes money

The listing says the app is free and includes in-app purchases, but no price, trial, paywall, or paid feature is provided. Its appearance on grossing charts confirms that the product is associated with grossing rankings, not the amount of revenue earned.

Who built it

The developer is listed as TEGS LABS LTD. The App Store listing dates the release to 2026-06-10.

What it does badly

The listing provides no acquisition evidence, monetization details, retention metrics, or local-language scope, and the chart jump cannot be translated into downloads. The product also had only two days of tracked history in the supplied data, limiting before-and-after conclusions.

If it were you

The opportunity is a localized, budget-first meal planner rather than a broad meal-planning clone. Start by validating one market and one repeated behavior, such as generating a weekly plan from pantry contents and a budget; paid conversion, repeat planning, sharing, and referral activation would prove the wedge. This verdict would be wrong if the chart rise came from a one-off campaign with little repeatable community or subscription value.
