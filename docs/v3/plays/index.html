Playbooks

Concrete moves that more than one product used recently and that you can copy. Induced from the products we investigated, then checked case by case: what each one did, what happened, how sure we are it caused the lift, and who tried the same move without a lift. Follow the play, not the category.

  1. 1
    Survey one underserved audience, identify the same recurring decision or workaround in at least five recent responses, then ship one focused workflow for that exact task and measure its adoption for 14 days.
    Repeated audience-specific pain can narrow product scope, but the evidence shows only that focused workflow updates coincided with rank gains, not that surveying caused growth.

  2. 2
    Give users one complete useful result free, then offer one paid plan at the repeat action; disclose price, renewal cadence, usage limits, and the exact unlocked capability before checkout.
    A free completed result can demonstrate value before asking for payment, but the available cases do not establish that this lock point caused revenue growth.

  3. 3
    Publish one before-and-after demo in the audience's exact community, show the completed result rather than a feature list, and attach a unique source link before publishing.
    A visible transformation lowers interpretation cost and creates a traceable path from attention to activation, but the supplied cases do not establish that any demo or channel caused their measured revenue changes.
