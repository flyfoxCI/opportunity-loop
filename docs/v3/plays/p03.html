#2 · Monetization

Give users one complete useful result free, then offer one paid plan at the repeat action; disclose price, renewal cadence, usage limits, and the exact unlocked capability before checkout.

A free completed result can demonstrate value before asking for payment, but the available cases do not establish that this lock point caused revenue growth.

5 products used it · 0 with a clear cause · last verified Nov 20

Who it suits, and when it doesn’t work

Suits products with an immediately understandable action such as organizing photos, creating a meal plan, tailoring an application, or producing a short video. It also suits utilities where a visible free allowance can reveal repeat intent without requiring a subscription decision at signup; it is unsuitable for products that cannot deliver a complete useful result before payment.

Prerequisites

Requires a free path that completes one useful result, one clearly defined repeat action, an exact paid capability, one price and renewal policy, and cohort tracking for completed free results, paywall views, purchases, refunds, and seven-day repeat use. The proposed payment trigger must be tested rather than presented as an evidence-backed rule.

Risks

  1. A free allowance may cannibalize paid demand
  2. An aggressive lock point may suppress activation
  3. Unclear renewal terms may increase refunds
  4. Store and payment rules vary by market
  5. Chart rank can conceal weak or short-lived monetization

Worth saying more

Across the cases, the recurring model is a free core utility with in-app purchases. The most specific free allowance is Organize fotos: FocusClean's “até 100 fotos gratuitamente por mês,” while Content Manager is the only case with a stated subscription period, “UM ANO GRÁTIS PARA ESTUDANTES.” No case verifies a post-result lock point, so that timing remains a hypothesis. SmartlyCV and TrendStory both show substantial source-reported 30-day MRR increases, while three apps reached grossing-chart positions. These numbers are not proof of the proposed offer: the SaaS cases lack prices and checkout moments, and the app cases lack revenue, conversion, and retention data.
