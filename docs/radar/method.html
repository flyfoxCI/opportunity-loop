Score v2

score = (0.30 money + 0.25 momentum + 0.20 demand + 0.15 gap + 0.10 fit) × confidence

  • Money: 70% log-scaled median MRR of the top 5 verified products ($300 → 0, $30K → 1), 30% percentile of top-grossing chart apps.
  • Momentum: percentile of products growing 30%+, moving products, young charting apps and trending repos.
  • Demand: percentile of buyer asks on Reddit per week and the replies they draw.
  • Gap: half “fewer players than other directions”, half “more asks per player”.
  • Fit: a fixed prior for a 14-day, $300 solo build.
  • Confidence: 1 source 0.5, 2 sources 0.8, 3 or more 1.0.

ENTER needs all four: score 0.60 or more, at least 2 verified products at $1K+ MRR (people already pay), demand 0.5 or more (people are asking now) and gap 0.3 or more (room left). NARROW is score 0.45 or more with the evidence floor met: a verified product at $500+ MRR, or activity in 2+ sources. Everything else is WATCH. A day with no ENTER is a valid result.

What counts as moving

  • TrustMRR: MRR up at least 1.3× and $100 between our snapshots within 45 days, or TrustMRR's own 30-day growth of 40–2000%.
  • App Store: a climb of 15+ places in 8 days, or an indie app under 6 months old already in the top 40 grossing of its genre. Launches by large publishers are skipped.
  • App Store ratings: daily new ratings at 3× or more their 7-day average.
  • GitHub: 150+ stars in a day.

History so far: 18 days of TrustMRR snapshots. Products tracked: App Store 2,824, GitHub 33, Web traffic 8, TrustMRR 492.
